Professional Overview

Cristian Basic is a mining engineer specializing in mineral resource estimation, geostatistics, geological data management, and the development of technology solutions for mining.

He has more than a decade of industry experience, contributing to deposit evaluation, mineral resource estimation and classification, block model validation, and the preparation of information for strategic mine planning.

His background spans mining engineering, geostatistics, programming, data science, and artificial intelligence. He combines established methods with advanced computational tools to address technical challenges.

His work focuses on technical rigor, data traceability, process optimization, and solutions that improve decision-making in mining projects.

Integrating mining engineering, geostatistics and artificial intelligence to turn geological data into well-founded technical decisions.

He combines mining experience with advanced computational tools to develop reproducible, traceable solutions tailored to the characteristics of each deposit.
